Threat Vector Taxonomy

Punycode & Homoglyph Attacks

Attackers register Internationalized Domain Names (IDNs) using Cyrillic or Greek characters (e.g. Cyrillic 'а' U+0430) that appear visually indistinguishable from Latin 'a'. Browsers encode these as xn-- prefixed domains.

Brand Typosquatting & Combosquatting

Combosquatting occurs when attackers pair legitimate brand names with security terms (e.g., paypal-security-verify.com). Typosquatting relies on character substitutions (paypa1.com, rnicrosoft.com) to deceive hurried users.

Credential Harvesting & Subdomain Masking

On mobile devices with truncated address bars, attackers place trusted brand names deep within subdomains (paypal.com.account-verify.ru), obscuring the true root domain at the far right of the host string.

Urgency & Psychological Coercion

Phishing messages manufacture artificial crisis (e.g. "Account suspended within 24 hours", "Immediate IRS legal summons") to trigger cognitive overload and bypass user caution.

APK Namespace Shadowing & Suffix Hijacking

Malware authors disguise trojanized Android packages by shadowing official namespaces (e.g. com.whatsapp.update.free) or faking core OS services (com.android.system.patch) to solicit Accessibility permissions.

Open Redirect & @ Masquerade Exploitation

Browsers interpret characters before the @ symbol as basic authentication credentials. Visiting https://google.com@phish-site.ru routes traffic directly to phish-site.ru.
